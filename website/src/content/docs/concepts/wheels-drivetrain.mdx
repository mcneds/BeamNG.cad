Visible wheel CAD and native tire physics are separate. A detailed tire mesh does not supply pressure, friction, carcass stiffness or compound behaviour.

## Suspension support loads

The static solver balances assembled mass and COM over the four contact points, choosing the admissible distribution nearest equal corner loads. Perturbing the linkage measures how moving masses change height with hub travel. That supplies effective unsprung gravity load without charging the same mass to the spring twice.

For sprung corner mass `m`, motion ratio `r`, frequency `f` and damping ratio `ζ`, the automatic setup uses:

```text
spring stiffness k = m (2πf)² / r²
damping c          = 2 ζ m (2πf) / r²
preload force      = m g / r
```

These are static setup calculations. Measured free length, installed preload and hardware rates remain separate inputs. Bounded damper travel and spring preload use separate reference lengths so preload does not engage a stop at design ride height.

## Pressure wheels

BeamNG pressure wheels supply hub/tread nodes, pressure groups, tire beams and contact. The verifier inspects the compiled wheel topology and effective collision flags.

The `cad_axisymmetric` mass option fits CAD total mass and axial inertia, with a reported approximation to transverse inertia. It uses the declared bearing axis and CAD COM rather than assuming a bearing-face datum is the tire centre. Infeasible positive mass/radius allocations are rejected.

Tire damping, precompression, bead width and hub stiffness are physical model settings that require a new verified package when changed. Native wheel-axis measurements help distinguish carrier alignment error from carcass deformation.

Contact resolution is separate from the suspension linkage. New vehicle generation defaults to 24 tire rays; the existing `tire_rays` parameter accepts integers from 12 to 32. A coarse rolling tire can excite visible wheel hop on flat ground even when a stationary vehicle settles normally. More detailed appearance meshes do not improve these native contacts.

The generator allocates CAD mass and evaluates the provisional carcass recipe at a 16-ray reference, then redistributes each ring's mass over the requested ray count. This preserves ring mass, centre and analytical inertia without adding suspension nodes or changing the shock laws. It also avoids applying the low-mass carcass budget again merely because the contact nodes are smaller. Refinement adds native wheel nodes and changes the discrete carcass: it does **not** prove identical effective tire stiffness or calibrated rubber behaviour. Verify rolling motion at neighboring speeds after changing resolution.

## Torque paths

The earlier geared-AWD preset is:

```text
Engine → centrifugal clutch → CVT → rear reduction → carrier takeoff
                                                   ├→ rear differential → rear wheels
                                                   └→ front output gearing → front differential → front wheels
```

The front output is upstream of the rear left/right differential, not taken from a rear halfshaft. The RWD layout omits the front driven branch.

The native **Driveline** workspace also supports arrangements authored from CAD
endpoints and typed device ports. Layout presets are starting points; graph
connections determine the actual torque path, driven hubs and housing ownership.
See [Driveline graphs and shaft sections](/guides/shafts/) for authoring and limits.

Reaction nodes bind the engine and differential devices to their physical housings. Braking reacts through uprights or trailing arms. The numerical torsional budget includes both geared wheel inertia and housing recoil; provisional coupling compliance does not calibrate real shaft twist, heating or failure.

Native verification checks device parents, ports, ratios, wheel directions and reaction bindings. Supported tests also reject repeated wheel-speed reversals that an average-speed check could conceal.

## Collision boundary

The wrapped frame is an external envelope spanning gaps between tubes. In a full vehicle it retains terrain/other-vehicle collision while disabling internal self-collision, avoiding false contact with attached parts inside that envelope. Contact with the actual individual frame members requires a different representation.

Read [Verification](/guides/verification/) before treating a generated vehicle as accepted.
