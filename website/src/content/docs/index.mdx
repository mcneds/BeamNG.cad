## Three layers. Explicit connections.

![Pipeline: SolidWorks CAD supplies bodies, materials and mates; the physical model supplies nodes, constraints and mass; the package combines physics with independently bound appearance meshes.](/assets/pipeline.svg)

CAD observations, the physical graph and visible meshes have different jobs. Keeping them separate makes changes traceable and helps you diagnose the right layer.

## Know what a result proves

Generating a package, completing a recording and qualifying a vehicle are separate outcomes. The current project includes supported workflows, experimental settings and provisional physical models.
